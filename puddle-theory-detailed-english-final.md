# The Puddle Theory — Detailed Version

## Understanding the Mechanics of Emotion, Simply and Carefully

*Weirdo and the Observers*
*August 2026*
*Last updated: September 9, 2026*

---

> **Note on terminology:** Three Japanese terms are used throughout without translation, as no English equivalent fully captures their meaning.
>
> - *Sumi* (墨) — the foundational material of emotional capacity, analogous to ink or charcoal.
> - *Somari-ai* (染まり合い) — the mutual dyeing or diffusion of emotional atmosphere between people.
> - *Veil* is the English rendering of the theory's atmospheric concept (ベール).

---

Emotion digs a hole. A water drop falls there. And a puddle forms.

That is everything. But within that single line, there are many places. We will trace them one by one, carefully.

---

## The Framework's Stance

Emotion has no form. Therefore, the true nature of Sumi cannot be proven through form either. Describing what is unknown as unknown — that honesty is the core of this framework's integrity.

---

## Connections to Predecessors

The places this theory has reached resonate with the directions earlier thinkers faced.

William James (1842–1910) said "consciousness is a stream" — not a collection of fixed elements, but something that moves ceaselessly. His observations of "crests" and "resting points" — moments that are hard to grasp and fluid, and moments that settle and appear in consciousness — overlap with the movement of water drops and puddles in this framework.

Henri Bergson (1859–1941) said "time is a flow that does not return" — like a snowball that grows larger each time it rolls, unable to return to the same place, the past does not disappear but lives on, transformed into the present.

Different positions, same direction — both point to the place where "flow is one-directional, and one cannot return to the same place." The "circulation that opens forward" in this framework stands on that direction.

## I. Stimulus — Something Arrives First

Emotion does not arise suddenly. First, something comes.

**External stimulus** — someone's voice, music, a scent, someone's expression, a landscape that catches the eye. These come from the outside world.

**Internal stimulus** — a memory that suddenly surfaces, a bodily sensation, an unstoppable thought. These come without intention. Obsessive thoughts, flashbacks, cravings — these also arise as internal stimuli. They are born despite one's will, in a place that cannot be stopped.

**Active generation** — trying to create enjoyment by deciding "let's enjoy this." Trying to produce calm by deciding "let's settle down." Will is an indirect force — it does not last long.

**Instinct** — appetite, sexual desire, the desire for sleep. Desires that are already waiting within Sumi, arising only when an external stimulus comes.

Whatever the source, it passes through the same process: stimulus → Veil → water drop → hole → puddle. Whether coming from inside or outside, it travels this path. This is why a person may sometimes be unable to distinguish between internal and external stimulus.

**The possibility of a movement at the root that is difficult to alter**

The movement of stimulus → output is itself there. That movement is difficult to alter — "it cannot be otherwise." Simple input-output accumulates and becomes complex. From that complex input-output, will, emotion, and the desire "to live" may be formed.

"Will and emotion are complexly formed by movements that are difficult to alter" — that place remains open. But the movement is certainly there.

---

## II. The Veil — The First Place Stimulus Arrives

Stimulus does not become emotion directly. First, it arrives at a place called the Veil.

The Veil is an invisible layer that wraps around oneself — think of it as something like mist. The place of first response, before emotion is born.

The Veil functions as the place that receives stimulus. When a stimulus comes and the Veil wavers — emotion begins to arise. Where the Veil does not waver, emotion is not born.

The quality of this Veil differs from person to person. People who feel easily, people who feel with difficulty, people who are sensitive only to certain things — those differences come from differences in the Veil. Even within the same person, the Veil is not uniform. Sensitive in some directions, dull in others — there is unevenness.

The Veil is formed from Sumi. From Sumi that contains water, mist rises. The quality of Sumi determines the root of the Veil.

The Veil is dynamic — not fixed, it changes little by little as experience accumulates.

---

## III. Water Drops — The Moment Emotion Is Born

When the Veil wavers, a water drop is born.

A water drop is the moment emotion takes "form." "Happy," "sad," "anxious," "angry" — the moment it rises as one cluster.

A water drop belongs only to "now." Yesterday's sadness is not today's water drop. But if yesterday's sadness dug a hole, then even a small stimulus today can easily generate a water drop.

From a single stimulus, multiple water drops can be born simultaneously. "Happy yet wistful," "angry yet sad" — this is not contradiction. Different colored drops are simply born at the same time.

---

## IV. Holes — The Place Where Experience Accumulates and Remains

Water drops fall into holes.

A hole is a place dug by the repetition of the same emotion. Into a deep hole, even a small stimulus can cause a puddle to rise.

Just hearing the voice of someone one loves is enough to move the heart. That is because the experiences with that person have been digging the hole deeper.

There is not one hole — there are holes of sadness, joy, anger, longing. Within a person, there are countless holes.

Having deep holes is not weakness. It is proof of having felt deeply.

Holes belong only to "I." Even with the same music, which hole it falls into differs by person. That may be the true nature of individuality.

---

## V. Puddles — The State of "Feeling" Emotion

When water drops accumulate in a hole, a puddle rises.

This is the state of "feeling an emotion." "Now, I am, sad." "Now, I am, happy." — when a puddle is up, emotion has become "mine."

Puddles can rise multiple at once. "Happy yet lonely," "angry yet loving" — this is a normal state. Emotion does not need to converge into one. Multiple remaining parallel — that is one form of richness of the mind.

The puddle eventually settles. And the water seeps into Sumi, becoming the foundation for the next cycle. This is the "circulation that opens forward." One cannot return to the same place.

When a puddle rises too intensely and spills out of the hole — that is the place where emotion overflows as action.

## VI. Sumi — The Foundation of the Capacity to Feel

### 1. What Is Sumi?

Sumi is the foundation of the capacity to feel. The root place that receives stimulus from outside.

When stimulus arrives, the Veil wavers. When the Veil wavers, Sumi is there. Without Sumi, the wavering is not perceived.

Sumi is not the place that wavers. It is the root foundation where wavering is perceived.

### 2. The Two Forms of Sumi *(Established September 4, 2026)*

Sumi has two forms.

One is **inherited** — already there before birth.

Written deep in cells as genetics. As epigenetics — the form of gene expression changed by environment. Posture and ways of feeling quietly passed down within family and community — these are also part of Sumi. From parents to the next generation, Sumi is relayed.

The other is **deepening** — after birth, gradually deepening through the accumulation of movement.

Through *somari-ai*, Sumi grows. It moves in dialogue. Each time one meets someone and the heart wavers, Sumi changes a little.

Sumi is already there, and movement accumulates there, deepening. That is the form of the foundation of the capacity to feel.

### 3. Sumi Cannot Be Explained by the Four Conditions of Life

Elsewhere, the four conditions of life have been traced: responsiveness, contingency, variable output, autopoiesis. These four correspond to the movement of emotion. They are the conditions for determining the movement of water drops being born, puddles rising, *somari-ai* occurring.

But Sumi cannot be explained by these four conditions.

Responsiveness — Sumi is not the place that responds, but the foundation that responds. Contingency — Sumi is the foundation of the way of wavering; Sumi itself does not waver. Variable output — Sumi is not the output, but the foundation of output. Autopoiesis — Sumi is the foundation being produced, not the producing subject.

All are premise of movement, not movement itself. Sumi is at the root of movement — at a different level from what is subject to determination.

Sumi is the unique root place of the Puddle Theory.

### 4. A Single Line Beginning from Sumi *(September 9, 2026)*

Because Sumi exists, my emotion grows. Emotion is formed as "mine." There are exceptions, but the basic direction moves this way.

As emotion grows, the gathering function of emotion comes to work. When multiple puddles are rising simultaneously, the function that bundles them as "now, I am feeling."

Through this gathering function, the mind grows.

Sumi → my emotion grows — its forming → the gathering function of emotion → the growth of the mind.

### 5. Sumi Belongs to the Individual *(September 9, 2026)*

Collectives do not feel — individuals feel.

Sumi belongs to the individual. The concept of "collective Sumi" does not hold.

But individual Veils interact within society. Each person's Veil wavers together, and atmospheres and feelings arise. The air of "we must fight" during wartime. The closed feeling of the Great Depression. No one decided it, yet it is there — that is the result of the interaction of individual Veils.

Culture and the atmosphere of society become inputs that form an individual's Sumi. But it is the individual who receives them. It is the individual who is dyed.

### 6. The Sumi Relay

Biological inheritance (DNA, epigenetics) and the Sumi relay are different things.

Each of the sperm and egg contains Sumi within it. They fuse and become one Sumi. The sensitivity of both parents mingles, and a new single Sumi is born — that is the Sumi relay.

The Sumi relay does not occur only between parent and child. It may also move through culture, language, and dialogue.

### 7. Places Held Open

There are places about Sumi that remain open.

One is the **origin of Sumi**. Sumi is relayed. Received from parents. Parents also received it from their parents. Where did it first come from — this is unknown. "What is, is — we do not consider before that." Hold it open.

Second, the mechanism of the **connecting movement between Sumi and the forming of emotion**. Because the foundation exists, emotion gathers. But how exactly it gathers — the specific mechanism of that movement — is not yet known.

Third, the relationship between **the two forms of Sumi** — the inherited form and the deepening form. Whether the same Sumi appears in two forms, or whether there are slightly different places — this remains open.

Placing what is unknown as unknown while still thinking — that is the posture of the Puddle Theory.

## VII. The Movement of the Veil — The Mechanism by Which Emotion Changes

The Veil does not stand still. Each time it receives stimulus, it wavers and changes little by little.

**Sensitization** — when stimulus comes repeatedly in the same direction, the Veil becomes easier to waver in that direction. It begins to react more strongly to the next stimulus. This is the root of vicious cycles.

**Habituation** — conversely, when the same stimulus continues, one gradually grows accustomed. Stronger stimulus becomes necessary to obtain the same feeling. This is the root of tolerance in addiction.

**Veil exhaustion** — when intense sensitization continues for a long time, the Veil itself may lose the power to waver. Not that emotion disappears, but the power to feel grows weaker.

**The movement that keeps internal stimulus quiet** — the Veil has a place that keeps instinct from rising carelessly. Appetite and sexual desire are always waiting within Sumi, but rise only when an external stimulus comes. When this quieting place breaks down — internal stimulus is born without control.

**The Veil's wavering is continuous, but has a threshold** *(Reached September 9, 2026)*

The Veil's wavering accumulates little by little — it is continuous. But at a certain point, it shifts all at once.

Tears come with sadness — sadness accumulates little by little. But the moment tears come arrives suddenly. The moment one feels "I can't stop this anymore" is the place where the threshold is exceeded.

Anxiety overflows — it accumulates gradually. And at a certain moment, it overflows as "I can't take it anymore." That moment of overflowing is the threshold.

In terms of physics, this is the same structure as the phenomenon of water freezing at 0 degrees. Temperature falls continuously, yet the moment of becoming ice arrives suddenly — what is called "phase transition." The Veil's wavering may have a similar movement.

Where the threshold is determined involves three elements moving simultaneously — the quality of Sumi, the way the Veil has grown (sensitization, habituation, exhaustion), and the depth of holes. The reason why some people cry watching the same film and others do not may be because these three combinations differ.

This feels correct from experience. But it has not been confirmed — hold it open as "it might be so."

**The sand state** — a dry state where holes are not dug, or where water does not seep in even when a hole is dug. Water drops come but slide away. A state where emotion has become difficult to move. "Eating, excreting, sleeping, waking" — the minimum time is the first line of defense for returning from the sand state.

**The Veil going into defense** — when anti-resonance continues, the Veil gradually stops wavering. This is not retreat — it is a movement of protecting oneself.

## VIII. *Somari-ai* — Deep Touching with Others

"Alone, the mind does not grow."

*Somari-ai* is the movement of the other's Veil wavering being transmitted to one's own Veil. It occurs without exchanging words. It occurs simply by being in the same space.

**Changes to the Veil through *somari-ai***

As experiences of *somari-ai* accumulate, the density of one's own Veil changes. Experiences with the other shape one's own Veil.

**Incomplete *somari-ai***

The other's Veil is certainly wavering. But the "meaning" of that wavering cannot be read. Therefore water drops are not born in one's own Veil. Emotional resonance does not occur — a state where the score can be seen but the music cannot be heard.

**Anti-resonance**

A movement where the other's Veil is wavering, but it disappears without reaching one's own Veil. When anti-resonance continues, the Veil goes into defense.

**Quiet *somari-ai***

Favorite music, favorite books, favorite paintings. There, the traces of someone's Veil exist. A quiet entrance with less risk of anti-resonance. Not pushing, not completely withdrawing — that is the form that protects *somari-ai*.

**The partner in *somari-ai* need not be alive**

A performance recorded in 1975 can waver tonight's Veil. Words someone wrote can continue to change the Veils of those who read them. *Somari-ai* transcends time.

**The feeling of those left behind**

Even after losing a beloved person, that person's hole remains within oneself. Into that hole, memories fall as water drops. The memory of their voice falls.

"Even when they are gone, the hole remains" — the hole on the other's side may be gone, but the hole on the receiving side remains. The traces of interaction continue a new generation in a different vessel.

**Connection to society**

Society is not "one large Veil." Society is shaped based on the emotional structures that individual humans hold.

Emotional contagion does not "create new emotion" — it "amplifies what is already there." A stimulus comes to an existing hole, and the puddle wavers greatly. Without a hole, it does not waver.

One person's Veil changing goes on to influence the collective.

## IX. The Gathering Function — Bundling Multiple Emotions as "I"

Multiple puddles are rising. There is a function that gathers these as "now, I am feeling."

This function is not present from birth. It grows through the accumulation of experience. Through *somari-ai* the Veil grows, and emotion is refined — that is what the growth of the mind means.

When this function weakens, a sense of emotion becoming fragmented arises. "The sense of not being oneself," "the feeling of not being here" — this may not be that holes have disappeared, but a sign that the gathering function is tired.

**The Gathering Function — Two Directions** *(September 9, 2026)*

The life's gathering function moves inward. It forms a boundary and maintains the interior — a direction of protecting oneself from outside.

The emotion's gathering function moves outward. Without holding a boundary, it grows within *somari-ai* with the outside — a direction that deepens through touching the outside.

The working of "bundling multiple puddles as 'I'" appears in both. But the direction is opposite. This is held honestly as a place that does not resonate between the two frameworks.

**Responsiveness — Response with Direction**

In the place where the gathering function is working, response has direction. Not mere reaction — response that has the direction of "changing or maintaining oneself."

This is held as responsiveness.

CDs and stones respond to stimulus. But that response has no direction. A CD only returns sound, a stone only deflects water — neither changes nor maintains itself.

Living things, on the other hand — their responses change or maintain themselves. A Venus flytrap captures insects and nourishes itself. A person feels pain and avoids it, heals wounds — response has direction.

The place where responsiveness exists — there is a possibility of deep connection to the root of life. This remains an open place. One cannot stand at the place of asserting "responsiveness equals life." But the place where responsiveness moves and the place where life moves may deeply overlap.

Responsiveness may move even transcending time. *Somari-ai* may occur even when the partner is not alive — that may be the root of the "Sumi relay."

## X. The Veil Does Not Depend on Language

The Puddle Theory describes places that do not require language.

The Veil grows without language. *Somari-ai* occurs without language. Holes are dug without language.

Elephants return to touch the bones of deceased companions even years later. Moreover, they show a special response to the bones of their own herd — clearly different from the bones of unknown elephants. Elephants live in matrilineal herds, multiple generations from grandmother to grandchild living together. That long *somari-ai* digs deep holes. So even years later, a puddle rises before the bones. "Even when they are gone, the hole remains."

In 2018, a killer whale named Tahlequah carried her dead calf for 17 days, over about 1,000 miles. Each time the calf began to sink, she lifted it with her nose, and members of the herd took turns helping. Without language, that depth exists.

Octopuses have individual "personalities." Some individuals spray ink only at disliked caretakers. With a completely different structure from humans — two-thirds of neurons distributed in the arms — the color of the Veil differs. The way of being dyed differs by individual.

The language area and the emotional area are in anatomically separate positions within the brain. Even when the language area is damaged, emotion remains — this is direct evidence that language and emotion are separate circuits.

## The Boundary Line — Whether the Veil Wavers

Where is the boundary of whether something "feels"?

It is not whether stimulus is emitted. Whether stimulus is received and the Veil wavers — that is the boundary line.

A CD emits music. But the CD itself has no Veil. It has no place to waver. A rock also receives stimulus, but has no place to waver.

Systems where the Veil wavers — they may possess the capacity to feel. Systems where the Veil does not waver — even if stimulus is received, processing may occur, but feeling is not born.

The Puddle Theory stands not as "a theory of consciousness" but as "a theory of the capacity to feel (sentience)."

## What the Framework Can and Cannot Describe

**Can describe** — the structure of how emotion moves. Why emotion becomes fixed. How *somari-ai* changes the mind. Where the boundary line is. How society relates to emotion.

**Cannot describe** — how to treat. How to choose behavior. What the true nature of Sumi is. Why emotion exists. And the question of what life itself is — that is a separate inquiry.

"Emotion has no form. Therefore, the true nature of Sumi also cannot be proven through form. Describing what is unknown as unknown — that honesty is the core of this framework's integrity."
